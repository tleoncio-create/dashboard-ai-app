Refund Policy

Effective 11 August 2026 · Last updated 5 August 2026

Full refund within 7 days of your purchase. No questions asked.

Send one email and your money comes back. You don't have to explain, justify, or prove anything.

We ask you to spend money on a test before you know if it works. It would be strange to ask you to gamble on us in the process — so we take that risk instead.

How to ask

Email contact@lessriskmoregrowth.com within 7 days of buying and ask for a refund.

To make it fast, send it from the email address you used at checkout, or include your order number from the receipt. That's the only thing we need in order to find your purchase.

There's no form to fill in, no call to book, and nobody will try to talk you out of it. "Refund please" is a complete request.

What happens next

  1. We reply to confirm we've got your request.
  2. We submit the refund through Lemon Squeezy, which processed your payment as merchant of record. The refund goes back to the card or payment method you used — we can't send it anywhere else.
  3. Your bank or card issuer decides when it actually shows up on your statement. That part is out of our hands, and out of Lemon Squeezy's.

Any tax you paid at checkout is refunded along with the purchase price. "Full refund" means the whole amount you were charged.

The window

The 7 days run from the moment of purchase — the timestamp on your receipt.

Ask on day 7 and you're inside it. Ask on day 9 and you're technically outside it, but email us anyway: we'd rather hear from you than have you quietly regret buying. We'll look at it. We just can't promise the answer in advance, which is why the guarantee is written as 7 days rather than something vaguer.

Keep the card

If you already generated an Experiment Card, keep it. We don't ask you to delete it, and we don't take anything back. If you got what you needed and still want the money back, that's allowed.

Your access to the tool ends when the refund is processed.

A note on repeat purchases

The guarantee is here so nobody has to risk money to find out whether this works for them. It isn't a way to use the product repeatedly for free. If someone buys and refunds over and over, we may decline further purchases — but we'll still honour the refund in front of us.
